Getting a model from Bambu Handy / MakerWorld

  1. Find the model on MakerWorld (in your phone's browser, or from Handy's share button, which opens it in the browser).
  2. Tap Download and save the .3mf (or .stl) to your phone. If you use Google Drive, save it to the phone itself.
  3. Come back here, tap Choose a file, check the colours, then tap Print.

Bambu files are re-prepared with the right settings for your QIDI printer automatically.
