Getting a model from Bambu Handy / MakerWorld

  1. Find the model on MakerWorld (in your phone's browser or from Handy's share button, which opens it in the browser).
  2. Tap Download and save the .3mf (or .stl) to your phone.
  3. Come back here, tap Choose a file, pick it, then Print on Q2.

The bridge re-slices Bambu files with QIDI Q2 settings, so they print correctly on the Q2.
